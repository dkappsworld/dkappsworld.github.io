Privacy Policy
Effective date: 23 November 2026
App package: com.esy.alarm
Monetization: Ads only (Google AdMob). No subscriptions or in-app purchases.

This Privacy Policy explains what information ESY Alarm (“we”, “the app”) stores on your device, what optional data may be accessed, and how advertising works. Core alarm features work offline and do not require an account.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to users of the ESY Alarm Android application published on Google Play under package name com.esy.alarm.

2. Data we store on your device

The following data is stored locally on your phone using Room Database and DataStore:

  • Wake / interval / event alarm schedules, labels, and settings
  • Dismiss challenge preferences (math, tap, shake) and difficulty
  • Optional wake-up check setting
  • Theme accent / unlocked reward preferences
  • Ad-free day timestamp (24-hour interstitial pause), if unlocked via rewarded ad
  • JSON backup files only if you export them via the system file picker

We do not operate our own backend for core alarms. Local alarm data is not automatically uploaded to our servers (there are none for core features).

3. Optional Contacts access

If you choose Import from Contacts, the app requests READ_CONTACTS to read birthday/anniversary dates from ContactsContract so it can create local event alarms.

  • Used only when you start an import
  • Contact details stay on-device for local reminders
  • Not sold, and not automatically uploaded to an ESY Alarm server
  • You can deny the permission; the rest of the app still works

4. Permissions (why we ask)

  • Exact alarms – ring wake / reminder / event alarms on time
  • Notifications – show alarm alerts
  • Full-screen intent – show the alarm screen over the lock screen
  • Foreground service (media playback) – play alarm sound while ringing
  • Vibrate / sensors – vibration and optional shake-to-dismiss
  • Internet / network state – load ads (Google AdMob) and consent messages
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) – used by Google’s advertising services when available

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

ESY Alarm is free and supported by ads. We use Google AdMob for:

  • Banner ads on the main screen
  • Interstitial ads after saving/editing an alarm/event or on post-dismiss summary
  • Rewarded ads for optional extras (extra tones, accents, 24-hour interstitial pause)

Ad placement rules we follow:

  • Ads are never shown while an alarm is ringing
  • Ads are never shown during dismiss challenges
  • No paid plans, subscriptions, or in-app purchases

Google may process advertising identifiers and device/ad signals according to Google’s Privacy Policy and your device ad settings. In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, a consent message (UMP) may be shown before personalized ads. You can open Ad privacy options in the app Settings when required by law/policy.

6. Analytics & crash data

The app does not include a separate first-party analytics product for core features. Advertising SDKs may collect technical data needed to serve and measure ads, subject to Google’s policies and your consent choices where applicable.

7. Children

ESY Alarm is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through ads or device settings, use your device/Google account controls or contact us.

8. Data sharing

  • Not sold by ESY Alarm
  • Shared with Google only as needed for AdMob / Play services when ads run
  • Contacts / local alarms are not uploaded to an ESY Alarm cloud

9. Data retention & deletion

  • Local data remains until you delete alarms/events or uninstall the app
  • Uninstalling ESY Alarm removes the app’s local database and preferences
  • Exported JSON backups are controlled by you (wherever you saved the file)
  • Ad identifiers follow Google / device reset or delete controls

10. Your choices

  • Deny Contacts permission and still use wake/reminder alarms
  • Change notification, exact-alarm, and full-screen intent settings in system Settings
  • Reset/delete Advertising ID in Android Settings
  • Use in-app Ad privacy options (when shown) and Privacy Policy link
  • Export or delete local alarms anytime

11. Security

Local data is stored in the app’s private storage. Network traffic for ads uses HTTPS. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure; please keep your device locked and updated.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or laws/policies change. The “Effective date” at the top will be revised. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
