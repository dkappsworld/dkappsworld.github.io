Privacy Policy

Group GPS

Effective date: 23 November 2026
Last updated: 23 November 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Group GPS (“the App”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our free mobile application. The App’s purpose is live group location sharing so a group Head can see members on a map.

Privacy contact: nehadkkumari2@gmail.com

2. Summary

  • We collect location to share your live position with your group.
  • We show ads (Google AdMob). That is our only monetization model — no paid plans or in-app purchases.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • You control sharing by leaving/ending a group and by changing location permissions on your phone.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you provide

  • Display name you enter when creating or joining a group.
  • Optional group name when creating a group.

3.2 Location data

  • With your permission, the App collects precise location (GPS) while you are in an active group.
  • Location may continue in the background if you grant “Allow all the time,” so sharing can continue when the app is closed or not in use.
  • We may also read approximate battery level to show status to your group.
  • Location is shared in real time with other members of the same group.

3.3 Device and technical data

  • A random identifier stored on your phone to help you rejoin the same group safely.
  • Basic technical information needed to keep the service working (for example online/offline status).
  • Map display may use map tile providers; those services may temporarily see your IP address when maps load.

3.4 Advertising data

  • The App uses Google AdMob for banner and full-screen ads.
  • AdMob may use advertising identifiers and similar data to show ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud.
  • See Google Privacy Policy for more details.

4. How we use information

  • Provide live group location tracking on the map.
  • Show member status (online, last update, battery, distance).
  • Enable create/join/leave/end group, remove member, and SOS alerts within a group.
  • Keep the service reliable and secure.
  • Show ads that fund the free App.

5. How we share information

  • Group members: Your name, live/last location, battery, and online status are visible to people in the same group.
  • Service providers: Hosting for the App service, and Google AdMob for advertising.
  • Legal: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect safety and rights.
  • We do not sell personal information and do not share location with unrelated parties for their marketing.

6. Data storage and retention

  • Group and location updates are processed on our secure servers to provide live tracking.
  • Location is kept as needed for live tracking and recent group state.
  • When a Head ends a group, that group is removed from active groups.
  • You can stop sharing by leaving the group, ending the group (Head), or turning off location permission in phone Settings.

7. Your choices and controls

  • Deny or revoke location / background location / notification permissions in phone Settings.
  • Leave or end a group inside the App.
  • Reset your advertising ID in phone Settings (limits personalized ads).
  • Uninstall the App to stop all local collection by Group GPS.

8. Children’s privacy

Group GPS is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the App, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

9. Security

We use reasonable security measures, including encrypted connections, to protect data in transit. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Only share group codes with people you trust.

10. International users

Our servers may be located outside your country. By using the App, you understand your information may be processed where our hosting provider operates.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
