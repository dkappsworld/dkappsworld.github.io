Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 11, 2026

Esy Vault is a "Local-First, Privacy-Extreme" application. We have built this app to ensure that your sensitive data—including biometric access—remains entirely on your device and under your total control.

1 Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Esy Vault ("we," "us," or "our") handles your data. Your privacy is our top priority. We have designed this app to collect zero personal information on our servers. We do not have accounts, we do not have a cloud, and we cannot see your data.

2 Information Handling

All data you input into Esy Vault is stored locally on your device:

  • Local Encryption: Your vault content (Photos, Videos, Notes, Passwords) is encrypted using AES-256 bit military-grade encryption before being saved to storage.
  • Location Data: If you choose to save a "Pinned Location" item, the coordinates are stored locally in your encrypted database and are never transmitted to any third party.
  • Zero Tracking: We do not use analytics, crash reporting, or any identifiers to track your behavior.

3 Use of Permissions

The app requires specific permissions to perform sensitive actions upon your request:

  • Biometrics: Used to securely unlock the app. Authentication is handled entirely by the Android System; the app never sees or stores your fingerprint or face data.
  • Camera & Microphone: To capture photos, videos, or audio notes directly into the encrypted vault.
  • Location (Fine): To allow you to tag your current location in secure notes.
  • Media Access: To import existing photos and videos from your device gallery for protection inside the vault.

4 Backup & Data Portability

We provide two methods for data safety:

  • Google Auto-Backup: A system-level feature that may back up your encrypted database. This is managed by your Google Account settings.
  • Encrypted Manual Bundle (Secure Export): You can create a full manual backup. This bundle is an encrypted ZIP file protected by a Backup Password that YOU choose. We cannot recover this password or unlock this backup for you.

5 Data Retention & Deletion

Manual Deletion: You can delete any item at any time. Standard deletions move items to the Trash, where they are automatically and permanently purged after 30 days.

Permanent Wipe: You can use the "Delete All Data" option in Settings to instantly wipe the entire local vault and all encrypted media files. This action is irreversible.

6 Compliance (2026 Standards)

This policy complies with the 2026 Google Play Store Data Safety requirements. We provide full transparency regarding all sensitive data access and prioritize local device security over cloud-based convenience.
