1. Executive Summary & Philosophy

Esy Vault was built with a single founding principle: Your personal data is solely yours. We operate under a strict Zero-Knowledge Architecture.

Unlike conventional cloud apps, Esy Vault does not maintain user databases, servers, or cloud sync backends. Everything you store—passwords, notes, photos, documents, and videos—remains encrypted exclusively on your physical device hardware.

🛡️

2. Zero Data Collection & No Internet Usage

We do NOT collect, store, sell, or analyze your private information:

✓
No Account Required: You can use all vault features without registering an email, username, or phone number.
✓
No Remote Telemetry: No analytics SDKs (such as Firebase, Mixpanel, or Google Analytics) are included.
✓
No Internet Permission: The app does not request Android's android.permission.INTERNET, ensuring hardware-level offline security.
🔑

3. Cryptography & Encryption Standards

All sensitive information is safeguarded with state-of-the-art cryptographic standards:

✓
AES-256 GCM Encryption: Sensitive notes, credentials, and media files are encrypted with 256-bit AES authenticated with GCM tags using keys stored in the hardware-backed AndroidKeyStore.
✓
Salted SHA-256 PIN Hashing: Your Master PIN and Decoy PIN are never stored in plaintext. We only store one-way cryptographic hashes locally on your device.
✓
PBKDF2 Password-Derived Backups: Exported backups are encapsulated in an encrypted archive using PBKDF2 with 100,000 Iterations.
📱

4. Permissions & Transparent Usage

The app requests the following device permissions only for user-initiated vault actions:

✓
BIOMETRIC / FINGERPRINT: Used on-device via Android's native BiometricPrompt for rapid and secure biometric unlocking.
✓
CAMERA: Used only when taking photos/videos directly into the vault, or silently capturing front-camera photos upon 3 consecutive incorrect PIN entries (Intruder Selfie).
✓
READ & WRITE STORAGE: Used exclusively when importing files into the vault or exporting encrypted backup archives.
✓
RECORD AUDIO: Used only when creating encrypted voice notes directly within the vault.
🗑️

5. Data Sovereignty & Shredding

You maintain 100% control over the lifecycle of your data:

You may export backups, recover items from the Recycle Bin, or perform a permanent "Delete All Data" wipe from Settings. A factory reset cryptographically shreds all files, keys, and relational database records permanently.
