Privacy Policy

Zero-knowledge · 100% on-device · No INTERNET permission

1. Introduction

Esy Vault is built on a zero-knowledge, privacy-first architecture. Your private notes, passwords, photos, videos, and files belong solely to you. This policy describes how the app handles data and matches the in-app Privacy Policy for this release.

2. Zero data collection & 100% offline

Esy Vault does not operate servers for your vault content and does not include analytics, advertising, crash telemetry, or tracking SDKs.

The app does not declare the Android INTERNET permission, so it cannot upload your vault or “phone home” by itself. No accounts. No cloud. We cannot see your data.

3. Strong on-device encryption

Vault content and media are protected with AES-256-GCM via Android Keystore / EncryptedFile. On devices that support it, keys can be hardware-backed (StrongBox/TEE); otherwise the software Keystore is used.

Your Master PIN is never stored in plaintext — only a salted BCrypt hash is kept locally.

4. What leaves the vault (only when you choose)

Nothing is sent to the internet by Esy Vault. Content leaves encrypted storage only when you start an action:

  • Share — temporary decrypt for an app you select
  • Clipboard — text or media you choose to copy
  • Export backup — password-protected chunked AES-256-GCM (ESYBK2) archive that you save
  • Open with — brief decrypt for an external viewer you select

Temporary plaintext share/open copies are wiped when the app locks or on cold start (not on every background transition, so Android Share sheets can keep working).

5. Permissions transparency

  • Biometrics — optional on-device unlock only
  • Camera — capture into the vault; optional intruder selfie on failed unlock
  • Photo / video / files — system Photo Picker / document picker; no broad gallery scan (READ_MEDIA_*)
  • Microphone — secure voice notes only
  • Location — only when you tap “Use current location” for a private location note; encrypted on-device; never uploaded
  • Notifications — optional local backup reminders and large export/import progress
  • Foreground service (data sync) — local-only progress for multi-GB backup/restore; no network

6. Intruder selfie & local logs

When Intruder Stealth Selfie is enabled, break-in photos and timestamps stay in the app’s sandboxed storage and are never uploaded by the app.

7. Encrypted backups

Exports use chunked AES-256-GCM (ESYBK2) with a PBKDF2-derived key (100,000 iterations). Android Auto Backup / cloud backup of vault data is disabled.

You control backup files and passwords. Prefer local Downloads/SD for multi-GB videos. Esy Vault cannot recover a forgotten backup password.

8. Data ownership & deletion

You own your data. Delete items, restore from Recycle Bin, or use permanent Delete All Data anytime.

9. Children

Esy Vault is not directed at children under 13.

10. Changes

We may update this policy with the app. Prefer the in-app Privacy Policy for the wording that matches your installed build. Republish this page when you ship a policy change.
