Privacy Policy · Last updated: November 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Tickchu Tools (“the App”) is an Android application that provides on-device image and PDF tools (resize, convert, compress, crop, background removal, PDF merge/split, and related features). This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use the App.

2. On-device processing

Images and PDF files you select are processed locally on your device. We do not operate a Tickchu server that receives, stores, or analyzes your media files.

3. Storage on your device

Processed outputs are saved to your device using Android MediaStore, typically under a TickchuTools folder in Pictures and/or Documents. You control those files through your gallery, file manager, or Android storage settings.

4. Permissions

  • Photos / documents: via the system picker (you choose which files to open).
  • Internet / network state: for ads and optional model download.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob for advertising purposes.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads through Google AdMob. AdMob and Google may collect and process data such as device identifiers (including Advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app activity, and ad interaction data, according to Google’s policies.

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings (typically under Privacy / Google / Ads). Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob & privacy.

6. Machine learning (background removal)

Background removal uses Google ML Kit Subject Segmentation. Processing runs on your device. The model may download through Google Play services the first time you use the feature.

7. Data we do not collect

We do not create user accounts. We do not intentionally collect your name, email, contacts, or the contents of your photos/PDFs on our own servers.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region). If you believe a child has used the App in a way that concerns you, contact us via the Play Store listing.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when features or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top will be revised when changes are published.

10. Contact
