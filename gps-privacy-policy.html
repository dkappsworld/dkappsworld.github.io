Privacy Policy

App: GPS Camera Photo with Location, Map & Timestamp
Last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app GPS Camera Photo with Location, Map & Timestamp (“the App”) accesses, uses, and shares information when you use it.

1. Developer contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer at: nehadkkumari2@gmail.com

2. Data we access on your device

  • Camera: to capture photos.
  • Location (approximate and precise, while in use only): to stamp GPS coordinates, altitude, address text, compass heading context, map overlays, and optional weather on photos. We do not request or use background location.
  • Photos you capture in this App: saved to your device gallery folder Pictures/GPSMapCamera, including optional EXIF GPS tags.
  • Device sensors (compass/magnetometer): for heading text on watermarks.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads (see section 5).
  • Network access: for map tiles, reverse geocoding (device Geocoder), optional weather, and ads.

3. What stays on your device

Captured photos, stamped overlays, and app settings are stored locally on your device. We do not operate an account system, and we do not upload your photos to our servers.

4. Limited network use (maps & weather)

When features that need the internet are enabled and a network is available, limited technical data may be sent:

  • OpenStreetMap tile servers: map tile requests (based on your location area) and your IP address, to download map imagery for stamps / manual location picker.
  • Open-Meteo: latitude/longitude to fetch current weather when the weather stamp option is enabled.
  • Android Geocoder / platform services: coordinates may be resolved to an address by the device OS / available geocoding providers.

Weather can be turned off in Settings → Stamp Visibility → Show Weather. Map overlays can be turned off via Show Mini Map.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to show ads and support development:

  • Banner ads on secondary screens (Settings and Photo preview). Ads are not shown over the camera viewfinder.
  • Interstitial ads occasionally after a photo is saved (limited frequency so they do not interrupt every capture).

Google and its partners may collect or receive information such as Advertising ID, IP address, device and app activity related to ads, and approximate diagnostics, to show, measure, and improve ads. How Google uses this data is described in Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob help.

You can reset or limit the Advertising ID in your device settings (Google → Ads / Privacy). On supported devices you may also use system ads personalization controls.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell or rent your photos or location history. Your photos are not uploaded or shared by us.

  • Map / weather / geocoding providers receive only what is needed for those features (section 4).
  • Google AdMob receives ad-related signals as described in section 5.

7. Retention and deletion

Photos remain on your device until you delete them (in the App or in your system gallery). Clearing the App’s map cache removes offline tiles. Uninstalling the App removes App settings; gallery photos you saved may remain until you delete them. Ad-related data handled by Google follows Google’s retention practices.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App is not designed for use by children in a way that would require parental consent under applicable children’s privacy laws.

9. Permissions summary

  • Camera – core photo capture
  • Fine/Coarse location – foreground only, for GPS watermarks
  • Internet / network state – maps, weather, geocoding, ads
  • Advertising ID – AdMob ads
  • Write external storage (Android 9 and below only) – saving photos

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after updates means you acknowledge the revised policy.

11. Google Play Data safety (summary)

  • Location: collected/processed for app functionality (on device + optional weather/map network calls)
  • Photos: created by you; stored on device; not shared by the developer
  • Device or other IDs (Advertising ID): shared with Google for advertising
  • No account system; we do not sell your personal data
  • Data encryption in transit: HTTPS for weather, map tiles, and ad requests
