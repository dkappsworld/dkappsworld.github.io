1. Application Overview

Loan Mukti is an EMI calculator and payoff tracker. It does not offer, arrange, or broker loans, and it is not a government loan scheme. Calculations allow 2 to 360 months and an interest rate up to 36%. Internet is used only to load ads.

This Privacy Policy explains how our application functions with respect to user privacy and data security.

2. Data Collection & Transmission

We believe your financial privacy is paramount.

  • Personal Data: We do NOT collect names, phone numbers, email addresses, identification numbers, or contacts.
  • Financial Data: We do NOT collect bank account numbers, credit scores, credit card numbers, or transaction histories.
  • Device and ads: Google AdMob may receive an advertising ID, IP address, and coarse device data to show and measure ads. Loan Mukti does not send your loan names, amounts, or payment history to Google.
  • Permissions: Notifications are only for EMI reminders you turn on. Internet is used only to load ads. The app does not read SMS, contacts, photos, or location.
  • Network: Loan data is not uploaded. A backup file leaves the phone only if you choose where to save it.
  • Your export: If you save a backup file, that file is stored only in the place you pick.

3. Data Storage & Security

All loan calculations, custom EMI records, and payment logs created within the app are stored exclusively in an internal, local SQLite database on your device.

  • Data remains under your physical and logical control at all times.
  • Android cloud backup is disabled, so the loan database is not uploaded with Google backup.
  • Uninstalling the app completely removes all locally stored data.
  • You can clear all app data at any time via Android System Settings.

5. Third-Party Services & SDKs

Loan Mukti shows ads through Google AdMob. There is no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK. Ads are not aimed at children. In regions where Google requires a choice, the app shows a consent form before personalized ads. You can also limit ad personalization in your phone's ad settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Loan Mukti is a personal finance tool for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, it is not in Google Play's Families program, and ad requests are not tagged as child-directed.
