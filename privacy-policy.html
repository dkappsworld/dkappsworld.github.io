Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

SalaryDhan ("the App") is a personal finance management tool. We are fully committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how the App handles your information.

1. Data We Collect

SalaryDhan does NOT collect, transmit, store, or share any personal or financial data with us or any third party. All data you enter — including salary figures, deductions, budgets, and expenses — is stored exclusively on your device using the browser's local storage mechanism.

2. No Cloud Storage or Servers

We do not operate any backend servers. There is no database, cloud storage, or remote service associated with SalaryDhan. Your data never leaves your device unless you manually export it using the in-app Backup feature.

3. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display ads. Google AdMob may collect anonymous device identifiers and usage data for ad personalisation. You can opt out of personalised ads through your device's Ad Settings. See Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Permissions

  • INTERNET — Required for displaying Google AdMob advertisements.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Required to check connectivity before loading ads.

The App does not request access to your camera, contacts, location, microphone, or any other sensitive permission.

5. Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. You can permanently and irrevocably delete all locally stored information at any time by going to Settings → Clear All Data inside the app. This action immediately wipes all financial records, profile information, and preferences from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

SalaryDhan is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes your acceptance.
