DKVid Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

DKVid (“we”, “the app”) is a local cinema capture tool for Android.

1. Data we access on your device

  • Camera — to preview and record video.
  • Microphone — to record audio with your video (optional if you deny mic).
  • Photos / videos storage (via MediaStore) — only to save recordings you create into Movies/DKVid. We do not browse your existing gallery.

2. What we collect / share

DKVid does not collect, upload, or share personal data with us or third parties. There are no analytics SDKs, ads, crash reporters, or accounts.

3. Data stored on your device

  • App settings and zoom cues (local preferences).
  • Recorded videos in Movies/DKVid (and temporary cache files during export).
  • Optional invisible watermark metadata embedded in the MP4 when you enable it (channel name you type).

4. Backup

Cloud and device-to-device backup of app data is disabled.

5. Children

DKVid is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the app to create content that violates local law.

6. Your choices

Revoke camera or microphone access anytime in Android Settings → Apps → DKVid → Permissions. Uninstalling removes app preferences; gallery videos remain until you delete them.
