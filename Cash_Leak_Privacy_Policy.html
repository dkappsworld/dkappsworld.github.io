Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Cash Leak mobile application (herein referred to as "Application") for mobile devices, which was created as an Ad-Supported app. This service is intended for use as is.

1. Data Collection and Usage

Cash Leak respects your privacy. The Application does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data, transaction records, or habits on any external servers. All information you input is stored locally on your device in a secure SQLite database.

2. Third-Party Services

The Application utilizes third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. These services are limited to:

  • Google AdMob: For serving advertisements. You can view their policy here: Google Privacy & Terms.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): For managing user privacy consent compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and regional policies.

3. Data Deletion and Erasure

Since your data is stored strictly locally on your device, you have complete control over it. You can instantly delete all transaction logs, habits, and configuration settings at any time by selecting the "Delete All Data" button inside the Application's settings panel. Uninstalling the Application will also delete all stored records permanently.

4. Children's Privacy

The Application does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take the necessary actions.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
