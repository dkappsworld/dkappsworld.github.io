Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

1. Data Privacy & Local-First Storage

EsyReport is a localized, privacy-first productivity tool. All documents, photos, and profile information are stored strictly on your physical device. We do not transmit, sync, or store your personal media or reports on our servers.

2. Biometric Sensors

We do not use or collect any biometric data. EsyReport does not access your fingerprint, facial recognition (FaceID), or other biometric sensors for any purpose.

3. Third-Party Advertising

To keep this tool free, we use Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain non-personal identifiers (like Device ID) to provide personalized ads and analytics. You can manage these permissions in your device's Google Privacy settings.

4. Permissions Usage

We request Camera and Storage access solely to capture documents and save generated reports. These permissions are used exclusively for core app functionality and are never accessed in the background or for other purposes.

5. Data Deletion Rights

You have total control over your information. You can use the "Clear all data" or "Delete Account" tools in the Settings menu to permanently wipe all locally stored information. Once deleted, this data is unrecoverable.
