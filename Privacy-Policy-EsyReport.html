Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 25, 2026

Privacy-First Commitment: EsyReport is a localized, privacy-first productivity tool. All reports, PDFs, photos, digital signatures, and profile inputs are processed and stored strictly on your local device. We do not transmit or store your personal media or reports on our servers.

1. Data Storage & Privacy

EsyReport operates on a local-first architecture. Any details you enter (such as Office Name, Employee Name, or Report Notes), signatures you draw, or photos you capture are saved in the app's secure internal database and directory structure on your physical device. We do not maintain any cloud databases or external servers to back up or sync your reports.

2. Location & GPS Data Transparency

EsyReport requires location permissions to stamp precise GPS coordinates (Latitude, Longitude, Address, and Accuracy) directly onto your generated field reports.

  • Foreground Access Only: Location is requested only while you are actively using the camera module. We do not collect or monitor your location in the background.
  • Smart Fallback: If GPS signal is weak, you may opt to use your last known cached location.
  • Zero Server Transmission: Stamped location data remains entirely inside your generated PDF/photo files on your device and is never sent to us or any third parties.

3. Offline OCR & Document Scanning

All Document Scanning and OCR (optical character recognition) text extractions are processed locally on your device using on-device machine learning models (Google ML Kit and Tesseract). Your scanned documents and extracted text are never uploaded to any remote servers for OCR processing.

4. Digital Signature Processing

Digital signatures drawn inside the app are processed temporarily to render the signature image onto the generated PDF report. Signatures are not uploaded, nor are they stored or used for biometric identification. You can add or clear your signature at any time.

5. Advertising & Analytics (Google AdMob)

To keep EsyReport free for all users, we integrate Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect and process device-specific data, such as your Advertising ID, to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements. This data collection is managed by Google Services and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Permissions Requested

  • CAMERA: Required to capture report photos and scan documents.
  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: Required for stamping location data on reports.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required by Google AdMob to load advertisements.

7. Data Deletion Rights

Since all data is stored locally, you have total control. You can permanently erase all files, drafts, settings, and generated reports by selecting "Clear all data" or "Delete Account" in the app's Settings menu, or by clearing the app data in your Android system Settings. Once cleared, this data is unrecoverable.
