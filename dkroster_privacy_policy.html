Privacy Policy

App: DK Roster (Android) · Package: com.dkroster.dk_roster
Last updated: 23 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Android application DK Roster (“App”) handles information. DK Roster is a duty-roster calendar that helps you view shifts, manage job profiles, and receive pre-duty reminders.

Summary: DK Roster stores your roster data only on your device. The App does not require an account, does not show ads, and does not send your roster data to our servers.

2. What the App does

DK Roster provides:

  • A calendar view of duty, night duty, morning duty, rest, and off days
  • A default 13-day duty cycle, and the option to create custom roster cycles
  • Multiple job profiles and an active-profile selection
  • Pre-duty alarms and notifications (for example 30 minutes, 1 hour, or 2 hours before a shift)
  • Full-screen alarm-style reminders with dismiss and snooze
  • English and Hindi language strings in the App interface

3. Data stored on your device

The App saves information locally using Android SharedPreferences (preference file name: dk_roster_v2). This may include:

  • Job profile names and settings you enter
  • Roster templates (cycle length, anchor date, shift definitions)
  • Generated shift schedule for the year
  • Reminder timing and whether alarms are enabled
  • Which job profile is currently active
  • Simple app state such as whether the welcome message has been shown
  • Optional language preference, if you set one

This information is created by you through normal use of the App. It is used only to show your calendar and schedule reminders on the same device.

4. Data we do not collect

  • No sign-up, login, or user account
  • No cloud sync of roster data by the App
  • No advertising or ad tracking
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services that send usage data to the developer
  • No access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or precise location

5. Permissions and why they are used

DK Roster may use these Android permissions for reminder features only:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — to show duty reminder notifications
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM — to fire reminders at the chosen time
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — to restore scheduled alarms after phone restart or app update
  • VIBRATE — vibration with alarm alerts
  • WAKE_LOCK — to help the device wake for an alarm alert
  • USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT — to show a full-screen duty reminder when appropriate

These permissions are not used for advertising, tracking, or sharing data.

6. Internet and third parties

Core App features work offline. DK Roster does not require an internet connection to build your roster, show the calendar, or schedule local alarms.

If you install the App from Google Play, Google may process download, update, and related Play services data under Google’s Privacy Policy. That processing is by Google, not by DK Roster’s on-device roster storage.

7. Backup

Android backup is enabled for the App (allowBackup). If your device backs up app data to your Google account (based on your system settings), local App preferences may be included in that device backup. You can control Android backup in your phone’s system settings.

8. Children’s privacy

DK Roster is a general productivity / scheduling tool and is not directed at children under 13. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. How long data stays and how to delete it

Your roster data remains on the device until you:

  • Delete a job profile or rebuild/clear shifts inside the App, or
  • Clear DK Roster storage in Android Settings, or
  • Uninstall the App

Uninstalling removes the App’s local data, subject to normal Android behaviour and any backups you keep.

10. Security

Because data is stored on your device, protection depends mainly on your phone’s lock screen, OS updates, and physical access to the device. The developer does not operate a cloud database for DK Roster roster content.

11. Changes to this policy

If the App’s data practices change (for example if online features are added later), this Privacy Policy will be updated and the “Last updated” date will change.
