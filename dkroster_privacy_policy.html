Privacy Policy

App: DK Roster (Android)
Last updated: 24 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Android application DK Roster (“App”) handles information. DK Roster is a duty-roster calendar that helps you view shifts, manage job profiles (including optional details and a job photo), and receive pre-duty reminders.

Summary: DK Roster stores your roster data and any job photo you add only on your device. The App does not require an account. The free App is supported by advertisements served through Google AdMob. Roster content and job photos are not uploaded to our servers.

2. What the App does

DK Roster provides:

  • A calendar view of duty types such as day, night, first night, morning, rest, and off
  • A default 13-day duty cycle, plus custom rosters (railway, bank, company, labour, etc.)
  • Multiple job profiles with optional company, designation, ID, phone, notes, and one job photo
  • Pre-duty alarms and notifications (for example 30 minutes, 1 hour, or 2 hours before a shift)
  • Full-screen alarm-style reminders with dismiss and snooze
  • English and Hindi language strings in the App interface

3. Data stored on your device

The App saves information locally using Android SharedPreferences (preference file name: dk_roster_v3). This may include:

  • Job profile details you enter (name, company, designation, employee ID, phone, notes)
  • Optional job photo: if you choose a picture, a copy is saved in the App’s private storage on your phone
  • Roster templates (cycle length, anchor date, shift definitions including first night where used)
  • Generated shift schedule for the year
  • Reminder timing and whether alarms are enabled
  • Which job profile is currently active
  • Simple app state such as whether the welcome message has been shown
  • Optional language preference, if you set one

This information is created by you through normal use of the App. It is used only to show your calendar, job cards, and schedule reminders on the same device. Job photos are not uploaded to the internet by DK Roster.

4. Advertising (AdMob)

DK Roster is free and is monetized only through ads. We use Google AdMob to show banner advertisements. Google may collect or process device and advertising identifiers, IP address, and similar technical data to deliver and measure ads, subject to Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob guidance.

You can limit ad personalization in your device’s Google settings (Ads / Ads privacy), depending on your Android version and region.

5. Data we do not collect ourselves

  • No sign-up, login, or user account in DK Roster
  • No cloud sync of roster data by the App
  • No paid in-app purchases — income is from ads only
  • No separate analytics SDK operated by the developer beyond what AdMob/Play may provide
  • No access to your contacts or microphone
  • No continuous access to your full photo gallery — only a photo you choose (via the system picker) may be copied into App storage for that job

6. Permissions and why they are used

DK Roster may use these Android permissions:

  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to load AdMob ads (and optional connectivity checks)
  • AD_ID — advertising ID used by Google ads frameworks where applicable
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — duty reminder notifications
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM — reminders at the chosen time
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — restore scheduled alarms after restart or app update
  • VIBRATE — vibration with alarm alerts
  • WAKE_LOCK — help the device wake for an alarm alert
  • USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT — full-screen duty reminder when appropriate

7. Internet and third parties

Roster calendar and local alarms work offline. An internet connection is required to display advertisements.

Third parties involved when you use the App from Google Play / AdMob: Google Play and Google AdMob under Google’s Privacy Policy. Your roster entries remain in on-device storage and are not uploaded by DK Roster to a developer cloud.

8. Backup

Android backup is enabled for the App (allowBackup). If your device backs up app data to your Google account (based on your system settings), local App preferences may be included in that device backup. You can control Android backup in your phone’s system settings.

9. Children’s privacy

DK Roster is a general productivity / scheduling tool and is not directed at children under 13. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. How long data stays and how to delete it

Your roster data remains on the device until you:

  • Delete a job profile or rebuild/clear shifts inside the App, or
  • Clear DK Roster storage in Android Settings, or
  • Uninstall the App

Uninstalling removes the App’s local data, subject to normal Android behaviour and any backups you keep.

11. Security

Because data is stored on your device, protection depends mainly on your phone’s lock screen, OS updates, and physical access to the device. The developer does not operate a cloud database for DK Roster roster content.

12. Changes to this policy

If the App’s data practices change (for example if online features are added later), this Privacy Policy will be updated and the “Last updated” date will change.
